Release Notes - Apache RocketMQ - Version 5.3.4

November 26, 2025 · 12 min read
Download the 5.3.4 release
  • Source: rocketmq-all-5.3.4-source-release.zip [PGP] [SHA512]

Below is a summary of the issues addressed in the 5.3.4 release of RocketMQ. For full documentation of the release, a guide to get started, please refer to Quick Start.
